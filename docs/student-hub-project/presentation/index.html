The Problem

Campus life is scattered everywhere

Students juggle a different tool for every need:

  • Activities & events live on social media posts and group chats
  • Applications happen over scattered forms with no tracking
  • Achievements and engagement are invisible — no portfolio, no proof
  • Services, housing, and community are fragmented across channels
No single source of truth

Students miss opportunities; agents can't reach the right audience; admins have no oversight.

No recognition loop

Participation isn't measured or rewarded, so engagement stays low.

Our Solution

One platform. Three roles. Zero friction.

Students

Discover & apply to activities, earn points & badges, build a public portfolio, shop the store, and join communities.

Agents

Publish activities, manage applicants on a kanban board, verify check-ins, and track performance with analytics.

Admins

Approve agents, moderate content, oversee finance, manage users, and maintain platform-wide lookups.

Backed by role-based access control and a single REST API shared by web & mobile.

Challenges & Solutions

What we solved along the way

Integrating many modules

Challenge: activities, store, community & services on one data model.
Solution: Clean Architecture with clear bounded contexts and a shared API.

8 people, one codebase

Challenge: avoiding conflicts across web, mobile & backend.
Solution: feature branches, pull-request reviews, and CI on every change.

Bilingual + RTL

Challenge: mirroring the whole UI for Arabic.
Solution: a central i18n layer driving direction and translations everywhere.
